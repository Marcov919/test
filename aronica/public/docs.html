Flow

Aronica is the physical hand of a personal assistant in Milano: car wash (pick-up & return, or mobile wash), waiting at home for a technician or courier, local pick-up & drop-off (pharmacy, keys, envelope; max 3 km), IKEA assembly and small handyman jobs. Business staffing is experimental.

  1. compile_task → the user's words ("Porta la mia auto all'autolavaggio sabato mattina e riportamela, Navigli, berlina, interno+esterno") become a structured job: service, typed params, window, duration, fixed price with breakdown, proof (e.g. photos of plate, before, after + GPS), open questions. mode: now (Adesso, within 3h) or scheduled (Programma).
  2. search_supply → who is actually available for that job, ranked by proximity, rating, reliability, service fit and acceptance, with how each partner does it. Zero means zero: no fake matches.
  3. create_job → the job exists; fails closed (with a reason, e.g. fuori ambito v1) if out of scope or nobody can do it.
  4. negotiate / auto_negotiate → A2A round on when (never on price): supplier agents check their partner's calendar and reply accept, counter (another slot) or decline.
  5. accept_quote → slot locked; send confirm_url to your human (one tap). Then a timed offer goes to the best partner (TTL 20s): accept assigns the job, decline or timeout cascades to the next one; an exhausted list returns the honest message.
  6. wait_for_update until done; get_job for the partner, status and proof; rate_worker.

Demo key: ak_demo_milano (personal assistant, human confirms). Experimental business key: ak_demo_business. Simulated in this build: supplier agents (server-side), seed partners, escrow/payments, KYC.

Grok (xAI) and other function-calling agents

xAI's API supports remote MCP tools with the same /mcp URL and Bearer header. For plain function calling, load ready-made schemas from /v1/tools.json (OpenAI format: aronica_create_job, …). Map each call to the REST endpoint in the table below.

REST

B=; K="Authorization: Bearer ak_demo_milano"
curl -s -X POST $B/v1/tasks/compile -H "$K" -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"text": "Porta la mia auto a lavare sabato mattina e riportamela, Navigli, berlina, interno+esterno"}'
curl -s "$B/v1/supply?text=autolavaggio%20sabato%20mattina%20Navigli%20berlina&limit=3" -H "$K"
curl -s -X POST $B/v1/jobs -H "$K" -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"text": "Porta la mia auto a lavare sabato mattina e riportamela, Navigli, berlina, interno+esterno", "agent_name": "Claude"}'
curl -s -X POST $B/v1/jobs/JOB_ID/negotiate -H "$K" -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{}'

Fail-closed, honestly

Aronica dispatches only the services in list_services, only in Milano. Lessons, beauty, medical, certified plumbing or electrical work (Capoeira teacher, nails, dentist…), other cities, or zero available verified partners return an error with this exact message, and no one is promised anything:

Siamo spiacenti ma al momento non abbiamo persone sufficienti a soddisfare la richiesta.

Error codes: unsupported_task, unsupported_skill, unsupported_city, outside_service_area (422); no_supply (409).
